Critical behavior confirmed by the user
The current filter for Excluded Posts means the active named temporary filter's selected MATCH terms. Count distinct matching categories and tags together. At 3, show posts matching 0, 1, or 2 selected MATCH terms. NOT terms and the AND/OR operator do not affect this diagnostic. With no MATCH terms selected, every source post is excluded.

Config search, Categories only, and Minimum posts control the displayed inventory and Select all shown. They do not directly change the excluded-post calculation. Changing the active filter, its MATCH terms, the matching requirement, or the post index invalidates calculated results and requires another click.

Earlier committed features include saved named temporary filters in the secondary filter bar, OR by default with a saved AND option, import/export support, NOT filters, and removal of warning functionality. Do not confuse temporary filters with destination assignment rules or Config display filtering.

Mergers feature added after commit 6392e2b

The user revised the architecture: use a secondary display array, not synthetic matching keys. groupTerms in state/mergers.ts gathers members into one bar at their first occurrence and supplies absent members as dimmed companions. Clicking members changes original selections independently. Original post data, destination assignments, AND/OR/NOT, Selections, and Excluded Posts matching requirements are unchanged.

Each group shows ONE unique-post union total. Individual counts are suppressed inside grouped bars. Minimum posts compares the union count; ungrouped terms retain source counts. Categories only still gates original terms first; a mixed group can bring tag companions into view, but a tag-only group does not independently pass that gate. Select all shown selects all displayed members including companions. Grouping applies to Config, MATCH/NOT rows in all views, article terms, and excluded-post terms. Definitions are editable/dissolvable without rewriting filter references.

Mergers loads the lightweight post index for exact totals and offers an unsaved combined-count preview plus a saved group's post list. Counts remain unknown until complete. In live data, Leadership category 4555 has 13 posts and leadership tag 957 has 14; their union is 26 due to one overlap.
